01 · The Problem

Twelve real testers, fourteen days straight

New personal Google Play developer accounts can't publish until the app passes a closed test: 12+ testers opted in continuously for 14 days. Then you apply for production and answer Google's questions.

My obstacles:

  • Few Android contacts. Lots of my friends use iPhones.
  • Testers who say "sure!" but never opt in.
  • Noisy, fast-banning tester communities (Reddit, WhatsApp, Discord).
  • Dozens of reciprocal swaps to track.
  • Google watching for fake engagement.

02 · The Setup

Agents track and draft. People test.

Riley ran recruiting at first: Play Console checks, Group management, outreach drafts, and the reciprocal checklist. Pip took over the tracking routines on Sep 9. Ivy flagged Play Console and tester emails.

  • Real people only. Automation tracked, reminded, and drafted, but never faked opens or installs. I played partner apps myself at a human pace.
  • Human-sounding outreach. Every post, email, and DM got a "copy humanizer" pass.
  • Only swap with apps that have both links: a Google Group link and a working Play opt-in link.
  • The same Google account everywhere. Blank Play pages turned out to be account mismatches, not "Play lag."

03 · The Steps

How it runs, step by step

  1. Created the closed Alpha track tied to a Google Group. The track was available Sep 2, and the 14-day clock started about Sep 3.
  2. Built a tester checklist targeting 15–25 real opt-ins, not the bare 12, to cover dropouts.
  3. Warm outreach first: friends, family, and existing web players, with the website link in every message.
  4. Posted in tester-swap subreddits (r/AndroidClosedTesting, r/TestMyApp, r/TestersCommunity, r/AndroidAppTesters). The Group went from 1 to 13 members overnight.
  5. Joined reciprocal-testing Groups, opted in, installed, then privately asked each owner to test back.
  6. An agent scanned Reddit (read-only) and WhatsApp communities for fresh English-language tests with both links, skipping foreign-language, non-US, Mac/Linux-only, and banking-type apps. On Sep 10 I joined 10 reciprocal Groups and contacted all 10 owners.
  7. A live swap tracker (need_join → need_install → installed → done_14d, or remove_no_reply, unavailable_country_US, pending_play_lag) with a 5-day rule: no owner reply in 5 days meant uninstall and leave.
  8. Automated routines: a daily 9am install tracker (opted in / installed / day X of 14 / Group size), Tue/Fri reminders, and a weekday 5-day cleanup.
  9. Sep 7: uploaded build 1.0.36 (target SDK 36) mid-test after an upload-key reset, and confirmed it didn't restart the clock.
  10. Gave real feedback to partner developers (a launch crash, a country block, missing US units, a dictation glitch). That built goodwill.
  11. Sep 19: all three Play Console checks green. Submitted the production-access form, thanked testers, and wound down reciprocal swaps fairly (26 partners contacted).
  12. Sep 21, 2026: production access granted.
LESSONS FROM THE FIELD
  • Aim for about 2× the required testers.
  • Installs, not Group joins, are the real bottleneck.
  • Pace outreach. Subreddits filter fast, and too many DMs can restrict an account.
  • Check country availability and account matching before blaming "Play lag."
