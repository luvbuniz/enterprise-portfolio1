01 · The Problem

Post everywhere, run everything else, keep it clean

I wanted to post consistently across X, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok while running everything else.

And I had a harder rule: keep kid-facing content and adult content completely apart. A kids' channel can't have maker talk, and a maker channel can't show my kids.

02 · The Setup

Drafts, gates, and a queue

Agents research, plan, and draft a weekly calendar. Every draft goes through a humanizer pass and brand and privacy gates, and nothing posts without my click.

This is my own-brand version of the Social Media Operations Agent blueprint I design for B2B clients.

  • No kids' names, faces, or voices. Ever.
  • I appear as "Amy S."
  • No age.

03 · The Steps

How it runs, step by step

  1. An AI research pass on what works in the niche. For example, an agent studied 8 AI creators' channels.
  2. A weekly plan: 3 core vertical videos (20–45s, 9:16) plus optional extras, each cut for TikTok and Shorts.
  3. One recording, two cuts: a kid-facing Wizlings version and an adult maker-story version.
  4. Drafted captions and hashtags.
  5. An approval queue. I approve, edit, or reject.
  6. Scheduling.
  7. A weekly scoreboard, using real numbers only.
