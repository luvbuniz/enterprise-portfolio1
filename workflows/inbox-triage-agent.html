01 · The Problem

The important email is always under the promo

I have several inboxes: personal, Buni, and real estate.

Closings, tester emails, job alerts, and promos all land together. Important threads got buried. A deadline is easy to miss when it's sitting between two sale emails.

02 · The Setup

A digest, not an inbox

Ivy works read-only by default. It builds a "Must-know" digest with an urgent, actionable section first: the thread, the sender, what's needed, and the deadline. It flags Play Console and tester emails, and hands action items to Riley (Organizer).

  • No trash, archive, reply, or send without my OK.
  • Urgent and actionable items go first, sorted by deadline.
  • Action items get routed to the right agent, not left in the digest.
  • Bulk cleanup only after I approve a list.

03 · The Steps

How it runs, step by step

  1. Define "important" per inbox. A closing email and a promo don't get the same weight.
  2. Pull a time window, for example the last 21 days.
  3. Group by topic (closing, app, jobs) and sort by deadline.
  4. Write a must-know digest with the next action for each item.
  5. Route tasks to the right agent. Closings to Reed, app emails to Pip, gigs to Quinn.
  6. Bulk cleanup (old job alerts, promos) only after I approve a list.
